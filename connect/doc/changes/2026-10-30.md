### Added

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, new section *Speech-to-text → Transcription queue*: the
  `connect.transcription.queue` mixin (`transcription_pending`,
  `transcription_requested_by`, `_queue_transcription()`,
  `_run_queued_transcription()`), the cron `connect.transcribe_queue`
  ("Connect: transcribe queued audio") and
  `connect.settings._cron_transcribe_queue()` — voicemail first, then
  recordings, one commit per record.

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, new section *Speech-to-text → Recording and voicemail
  forms*: the *Transcription queued* banner, and the **Transcribe** button
  hidden while a record is queued.

- `doc/admin_guide.md`, section *Transcription*: the paragraph
  **Transcription runs in the background** — the scheduled action, the need
  for cron workers, and that voice messages are still transcribed on receipt.

- `doc/user_guide.md`, section *Call history and recordings*: transcripts appear a
  little after the call, *Transcription queued*, and **Transcribe** notifies
  you when it is done.

### Changed

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, sections *Call recordings* and *Voicemail*:
  `get_transcript()` and `create()` now only queue; the checks moved to
  `_check_transcription()`, and `_transcribe_queued()` re-checks the settings
  and transcribes in the cron. A recording without a media URL is now skipped
  with a warning on the automatic path instead of raising.

- `doc/admin_guide.md`, section *Transcription*: *Transcript calls* and
  voicemail are transcribed "shortly after", no longer "as soon as", the audio
  arrives.

```diff
 connect.recording.create()
-  get_transcript(fail_silently=True)  -> download + speech-to-text + summary, in the webhook
+  get_transcript(fail_silently=True)  -> transcription_pending = True, trigger cron
+cron connect.transcribe_queue -> _cron_transcribe_queue()
+  voicemails, then recordings -> _run_queued_transcription() -> _transcribe_queued()
```
