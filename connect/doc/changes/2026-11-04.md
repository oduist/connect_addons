### Added

- `doc/user_guide.md`, new section *Parking a call*: park on `*701`–`*710`,
  pick up on `701`–`710` from any phone, the caller returns to the slot when
  the pick-up is not answered, and one-key park keys on desk phones.

- `doc/admin_guide.md`, new section *Call parking*: the seeded slot
  extensions; a table of park or pick up by how the phone reaches the slot
  (transfer to `*70X` or `70X` parks, a Connect user dialing `70X` or `*70X`
  picks up, an outside caller sent to `*70X` is parked); the Yealink T34W
  one-key park setup (Call Park Mode *Transfer*, Call Park Code `*`, a
  *Call Park* line key with the slot as value).

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, new section *Call parking*: seed data, the parking
  fields on `connect.call`, the park-or-retrieve rules, `park_call`,
  `unpark_call` (dial-back, slot claim, queue-bridge fallback), the dial-back
  TwiML, `on_park_retrieve_action`, and clean-up at end of call. *Scope and
  status* now lists call parking.
