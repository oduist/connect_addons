### Added

- `user_guide.md`: first end-user guide for the `connect` module — where the
  menus are, the tray phone panel (keypad, favorites, history), placing and
  receiving calls, in-call controls (mute, DTMF, forward), personal phone
  settings on the user record, the call log and recordings, SMS/WhatsApp, what
  Connect adds to a contact form, and a short troubleshooting list.
- `admin_guide.md`: first administrator guide — the access model (Admin, User,
  Webhook and the separate "Do not record" privilege), General settings, Twilio
  and OpenAI credentials, the Calls tab, the S3 recording-storage checklist
  including the manual final step in the Twilio Console, transcription and its
  rules, the chatter prompt, the developer-mode request-verification switch,
  the Voice and Messaging configuration menus, the two scheduled jobs,
  licensing, and a post-setup health check.

Both guides are visible in Connect Book at `Connect > Documentation`; until now
the `connect` module had no page there at all.
