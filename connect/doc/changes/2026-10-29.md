### Added

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, section *Speech-to-text → Call recordings*: the
  `connect.transcription_rule` model and `check_rules()` — a call is
  transcribed automatically when at least one rule's calling and called
  patterns are both found (`re.search`) in its numbers. An empty rule list
  matches nothing, and the **Transcribe** button ignores the rules. The
  *Scope and status* section now lists transcription rules as covered.

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, section *Speech-to-text → Audio download*:
  `connect.settings.get_twilio_media_auth()` — Basic-auth credentials (Account
  SID and Auth Token, or the region token on a regional host) for media on
  Twilio's API hosts only, never for S3 or other URLs.

- `doc/admin_guide.md`, section *Transcription*, *Limits worth knowing*: the
  **Protected media** item — transcription works when the Twilio account
  enforces HTTP auth on media URLs, and a bad token shows as a 401 on the
  recording.

### Changed

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, section *Speech-to-text → Audio download*: the
  recording, voicemail and voice-message downloads now send
  `auth=get_twilio_media_auth(url)` instead of being anonymous GETs, which
  failed with 401 on accounts that protect their media.

### Fixed

- `doc/admin_guide.md`, section *Transcription*, group *Transcribe Rules*:
  said "Leave it empty to transcribe every call", but an empty list
  transcribes nothing. The group now explains how a rule matches, that
  *Transcript calls* needs at least one rule, that one `.+` / `.+` rule
  transcribes every call, and that the **Transcribe** button ignores the
  rules.
