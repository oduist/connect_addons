### Added

- `doc/tech_spec.md`: new. The module had no spec at all. This first one covers
  the softphone — composition, the screen chain, the dial and forward rules,
  the public methods the client calls with their gates and payload shapes, the
  TwiML parameters on a client leg, the component bus, the recent list, the
  asset bundles and the audio error handler. Its opening section says plainly
  what is **not** yet covered — the call and channel ledger, call flows, TwiML,
  recordings and S3, voicemail, transcription, SMS/WhatsApp, webhooks and
  settings — so silence in the file is not read as "no behaviour".

- `doc/user_guide.md`, section *The phone panel*: the panel moves, follows the
  Odoo colour scheme, and says in its header which extension you are and how
  long the current call has run. The tabs are now Keypad, Recent and
  Favourites, and they hide during a call.

- `doc/user_guide.md`, section *Receiving calls*: a call that reaches you
  through a colleague now names them under the caller — *Transferred by* — while
  the caller shown stays the customer.

### Changed

- `doc/user_guide.md`, section *The phone panel*: describes the live match
  under the dial field, search results replacing the keys, the SMS and WhatsApp
  buttons that appear once something is typed, and the caller ID shown under
  the favourites grid.

- `doc/user_guide.md`, section *Making a call*: rewrites the in-call controls —
  Forward, Keypad, Contact, Mute (Sound before the call is answered), End call
  — and explains that the forward picker and the tones keypad keep the call in
  a strip at the top with *Back to the call*, and that a forward changes
  nothing on screen until the server has accepted it.

- `doc/user_guide.md`, section *Receiving calls*: rewritten around the incoming
  screen — name, photo, number, open or create the contact, mute the ringing,
  Answer and Decline — rather than the notification alone.

- `doc/user_guide.md`, section *Call history and recordings*: opens with the
  Recent tab — grouped by day, direction and outcome on one line, search, click
  to call back, avatar to open the record, star to keep the number.

### Fixed

- `doc/user_guide.md` and `doc/tech_spec.md`, section *Recent list*: a call
  that did not connect now says why, and says it from the side you were on.
  The list had two words for every unconnected call -- *Missed* when it came to
  you, *Failed* when you placed it -- so declining a call on the softphone, a
  busy line and a call that rang out were all reported as *Failed* to the
  person who called. The provider reports a softphone **Decline** as `busy`,
  which now reads *Declined* to the person who pressed it and *Busy* to the
  person who called them; `no-answer` reads *Missed* or *No answer*, `canceled`
  *Missed* or *Cancelled*, and *Failed* is left for calls that really could not
  be placed. The spec carries the full table.

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, section *Recent list*: starring a call now records who it
  was about, not just the number. Starring an internal call stored the
  extension as a plain name and dropped the colleague, so Favourites -- which
  reads the name and the face from the contact or the colleague behind the
  entry -- showed an anonymous number. The contact, then the colleague, then
  the bare number, which is the order the list itself resolves.

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, section *TwiML parameters on a client leg*: records how
  the transferrer is resolved, and that the caller side counts. A transfer out
  of a call the agent had placed named nobody -- the agent is the caller on
  their own leg, the lookup read only called legs, and the field that would
  have covered it is not written until the call is finalized -- so the
  recipient of an outbound-call transfer was told nothing.

### Changed

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, new section *Softphone — size*: the panel is drawn at
  380x700 and shown 1.5x smaller through one `zoom` on the root, so the whole
  design scales together. Records the two calculations that had to follow it
  into the other coordinate space -- the `vh` height clamp, and the drag, which
  also still carried the bounds of an older, larger panel.

- `doc/tech_spec.md`, section *Softphone — size*: the panel shows at four
  fifths rather than two thirds, and its type ramp is drawn for that scale.
  Labels had landed around 9px, which is not a reading size; the smallest ones
  gain a third, and row and heading padding comes in to meet them so the list
  keeps its density.
