### Added

- `user_guide.md`: first end-user guide — the Calls button on a ticket, how a
  call finds its ticket by normalised number, creating or linking a ticket from
  a call, unlinking, and finding a ticket by phone number.
- `admin_guide.md`: first administrator guide — the module has no settings of
  its own, what it adds to tickets and calls, how automatic linking behaves
  (normalised match, never overwrites an existing link, never creates tickets by
  itself), the `connect_helpdesk` license check and its silent failure mode, and
  a health check.
