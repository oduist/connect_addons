### Changed

- A contact the agent creates takes its phone number from the call itself --
  the number dialled on an outgoing call, the caller's number on an incoming one
  -- instead of relying on the agent to repeat it back correctly.
- Orders created by the agent fill in the invoice and delivery addresses from
  the customer when the installed Sales modules provide those fields.
- The `create_partner` tool is told explicitly not to create a contact for a
  customer who is already registered.
