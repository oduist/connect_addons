### Added

- **Voice AI agents.** An agent answers a number or an extension and holds a
  conversation with the caller, with its prompt, voice and model parameters
  configured in Odoo. The call record carries the agent, the conversation
  summary, the transcript and a player for the audio.
- **Tools**, the mechanism by which an agent does something rather than only
  talking, and the calendar tools built on it: read a person's free time, book a
  meeting, list a contact's meetings and cancel one, so a caller can arrange an
  appointment during the call.
